§ I

The Instrument

A telescope makes the night sky legible. This instrument does the same for the mathematics of form, rendered at the precision its proofs demand. It disappears into what it shows: the canvas takes most of the screen, and every control waits at the edge until you reach for it.

§ III

Lineage.

The figures in this studio are not invented. They are inherited. Six of the people we owe.

1611

Johannes Kepler

Discovers the rhombic dodecahedron and sets out the harmonic geometry of the Platonic solids. The Mysterium Cosmographicum maps the inner planets onto the dodecahedron-icosahedron pair.

Harmonices Mundi · 1619
1879

Felix Klein

Defines the Klein quartic and its automorphism group PSL₂(7), achieving the Hurwitz upper bound of 84(g − 1) = 168 symmetries on a genus-3 Riemann surface.

Über die Transformation siebenter Ordnung · Math. Ann. 14
1931

Heinz Hopf

Constructs the fibration S¹ → S³ → S². Each point of the base S² lifts to a great circle of S³; in stereographic projection, the great circles become linked rings, the Hopf invariant.

Über die Abbildungen der dreidimensionalen Sphäre · Math. Ann. 104
1974

Roger Penrose

Constructs the kite-and-dart aperiodic tiling, proving that fivefold symmetry can tile the plane without translational periodicity. The kitchen-floor problem solved fifty years before quasicrystals were discovered.

The Role of Aesthetics in Pure and Applied Mathematical Research
1980

Benoît Mandelbrot

Iterates z² + c on an IBM mainframe and prints the boundary of what would become the most famous mathematical object of the late 20th century. The fractal nomenclature follows.

Fractal Aspects of the Iteration of z → λz(1 − z) for Complex λ and z
2023

Smith, Myers, Kaplan, Goodman-Strauss

Identify the Hat, a single tile that aperiodically tiles the plane. Three months later, the Spectre, a chiral aperiodic monotile. The decades-long search for an einstein, ended in a year.

An Aperiodic Monotile · arXiv:2303.10798
§ IV

The mathematics.

The studio is, structurally, a small library of theorems being evaluated against the canvas. Eleven of the methods being computed, by domain: the equations the kernel actually iterates, with the canonical citations. Calibration cards, not a textbook.

Escape-time iterationMandelbrot family · 2D escape time

zn+1 = zn² + c ; escape at |z| > 2 Each pixel is a point c ∈ ℂ; the GPU kernel iterates until escape or until maxIter. Burning Ship folds |Im(z)| on the imaginary axis; Tricorn conjugates; M3 / M4 raise the exponent to 3 and 4; Phoenix adds a second-order memory term p · zn−1; Celtic folds |Re(z² )|; Lambda iterates z(1−z); Buffalo and Newton complete the family.
Mandelbrot 1980
Ann. NY Acad. Sci. 357

Deep-zoom perturbation10⁶⁰-zoom interior renders

Δn+1 = 2·Zn·Δn + Δn² + δ A reference orbit is iterated in 256-bit fixed-point on the CPU; every other pixel is rendered as a perturbation Δ against it. Past 10²⁵ the kernel switches to a degree-2 Taylor series approximation; past 10⁶⁰, degree-3. Glitch detection rebases the reference when cancellation collapses |Z + Δ| toward zero (Pauldelbrot's criterion). Burning Ship, Celtic and Phoenix carry their own δ-recurrences past 10¹³ (fold-exact |·| arithmetic; Phoenix's memory term tracked exactly), and every integer power 2–8 of the z-family gets the generalized binomial recurrence.
Pauldelbrot 2014
fractalforums.com

Newton iterationroots of unity, 5 fractals

zn+1 = zn − f(zn) / f'(zn) For f(z) = zn − 1 the iteration converges to one of n roots of unity; each pixel colored by which root it lands on, lightness encoded as iteration count. The Cayley variant uses a non-rational f with three real fixed points and a basin-of-attraction structure first studied by Hubbard & Schleicher.
Hubbard–Schleicher 1986
Compl. Var. Theory Appl. 8

The complex-valued Descartes formula recovers the new circle's center z4; the recursion is depth-capped and produces a residual set of Hausdorff dimension 1.30568673…, the Apollonian dimension computed by McMullen via thermodynamic formalism.

§ VII

Keep it
with you.

AEON runs in your browser with nothing to install. Open the studio, then choose Install in the address bar (Chrome, Edge) or File, Add to Dock (Safari). The installed app opens in its own window and works offline.

To run it from your own computer instead, download aeon-web.zip and serve the unzipped folder from any local web server; opened straight from disk, its workers and offline mode cannot start. Every version is on the releases page.

§ VIII

What's new
in 7.0.1.

Smooth color climbs to the set · 2026-10-05

  • Smooth coloring now climbs from the palette's first color out to the set (AEON-2013, AEON-1993). The color spread of every smooth-colored fractal changes. The old curve used up the whole palette in the first few escape steps, so the background filled with rings of every color, then it jumped back to an early color near the set, leaving the palette's last color hard against a dark band. With Repeat above 1 that jump showed as thin black arcs. Now the colors climb smoothly from the first color in the far background to the last color at the edge of the set, with no jump. Ultraviolet on the whole Mandelbrot set shows it most: before, violet and white rings filled the whole frame; now the background is a deep violet that brightens into a glow around the set. The GPU, CPU, deep-zoom and WebGPU paths all match. Collatz opens closer in, where its color spread now shows. Known issue: where the browser refuses a preserved drawing buffer, the first Copy Image after a view change copies the view the app opened with (AEON-2036).
  • The canvas no longer starts as a square (AEON-1934). Until the first fit ran, the picture's frame used the old square boot layout, so a slow start or a screenshot taken at boot showed one square picture before it widened to fill the window. The frame is full-window from the first paint.
  • Growth patterns show their finished state in thumbnails and layers (AEON-1998). DLA and the cellular automata that finish in a background worker (elementary CA, Life, Brian's Brain, Day and Night, Gray-Scott) used to be captured from their quick first preview, so a catalog card, a layer thumbnail or a layer in a composite showed partial growth. Those captures now wait for the finished pattern.
  • AEON is credited to 7th Frontier, Inc. alone (AEON-2028, AEON-2029, AEON-2030). The Foreword now ends with "© 2026 7th Frontier, Inc. All Rights Reserved." The landing page, the catalog poster and the design records credit the company instead of individuals, and a new lint:attribution check fails if a personal or AI credit returns. The two home-screen shortcuts say "Open AEON" instead of the old name.
  • The Log Map formula preset draws again (AEON-2025). It started every point at z = 0, where log is undefined, then settled on one value, so the frame was a single flat color. It now starts at z = c and is colored by the angle of the point it settles on, which shows how that point turns with c and the cut where log jumps.
  • Two catalog entries no longer open nearly blank (AEON-2023). The Domain-coloring log formula preset painted the whole frame one color, because log(z²+c) settles instead of escaping. It is now colored by the angle of the point it settles on, which shows the branch cut and the bulbs. The Pickover attractor opened as a small speck in the middle of the window; its default view now frames it.
  • Biomorph draws Pickover's creature instead of the Julia set (AEON-2022). The type iterated z²+c with a square bailout and never applied Pickover's coloring test, so it rendered as the Julia set for the same c. It now iterates z³+c and paints a point as part of the organism when, at escape, its real or imaginary part is still inside the bailout radius, which grows the body's spines. The default bailout is 10, Pickover's own value. Saved views of Biomorph change look.
  • AEON-1656 and AEON-1876: the clipboard-copy and export-framing checks frame a content view, boot on a fixed seed, and declare the stale fallback copy against AEON-2036.
