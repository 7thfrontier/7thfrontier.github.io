Point-density exports match the screen at any page shape · 2026-10-18

- **Extend exports of point-density fractals match the screen's brightness.** A Buddhabrot exported with Extend at a shape unlike the screen's, such as a portrait page from a landscape window, came out about 17 levels brighter than the screen. Its samples and tone were scaled by the page's area, but these fractals scale the view by the page's short side. Quick, Print and layered exports of the Buddhabrot, IFS, strange attractors and the fractal flame now scale by the short side; Letterbox exports, and any export shaped like the screen, are unchanged.
- **Motion Studio exports of point-density fractals match the screen's brightness at any preset.** A Buddhabrot recorded at the Vertical preset from a landscape window came out about 14 levels brighter than the screen, for the same reason. Video and PNG-sequence frames now scale by the short side too.
