Drawings match their mathematics · 2026-10-07

- **Pattern notes match the pictures.** About thirty notes described something the picture does not show. The Perpendicular Mandelbrot's mirror is the real axis; the Lyapunov fractal's chaos is drawn in red and its swallows are the stable regions; Newton on z³ − 2z + 2 has whole regions that reach no root; the {9/2} enneagram is a single star. Symmetry claims for the Burning Ship and Tricorn Julia sets and the Buffalo, and the stated parameters of the Mandelbox, Buddhabrot, Nova, Thomas, Dadras and Duffing systems, now match what is drawn. The landing page's Penrose substitution rule is corrected the same way.
- **The Sri Yantra is drawn from its real construction.** Its nine triangles are now solved so that every point where three lines should meet is a single point, and their intersections form exactly the 43 small triangles of the traditional diagram. The pattern notes and References name the sources, Huet (2002) and Chiodo (2021).
- **The Penrose kite-and-dart tiling is a real one.** The P2 entry drew triangles that never assembled into kites and darts: its pieces met in mismatched edges across the whole picture. It now follows the true substitution, so every kite and dart meets its neighbors edge to edge, and kites outnumber darts by the golden ratio as they should. P2, P3 and the plain Penrose entry also draw each tile whole, without the line that used to run down the middle of every kite, dart and rhombus.
- **Elliptic curves close at the axis.** The curve y² = x³ + ax + b showed a gap wherever it crossed the x-axis, and its oval was drawn as two separate halves. Each component is now drawn as one unbroken stroke through its axis crossings. The exponential map now follows the orbit of its one special value, c, the standard picture of this family, and its description no longer claims critical points it does not have. The Lorenz and superformula notes now say what the controls really set.
- **The snub dodecahedron and the great stellated dodecahedron are the real solids.** The snub dodecahedron's edges varied in length by up to 84%, so its triangles and pentagons were uneven; every edge is now the same length. The great stellated dodecahedron drew the same 60 lines as the compound of five cubes; it now draws its own 30 edges, which outline twelve five-pointed stars.
- **Torus links and the Borromean rings cross correctly.** A torus knot whose two numbers share a factor, such as (2, 4), is a link of several rings, but only one of them was drawn; all of them are now. The Borromean rings had one crossing of each pair cut on both rings and the other on neither; now each ring passes over one neighbor and under the other, the arrangement that makes the three inseparable.
- **Lissajous knots are knots.** Most of the Lissajous knot choices drew curves that passed through themselves, which a knot cannot do. The four choices are now published examples, each a real knot: the three-twist knot, the stevedore knot, the square knot and 8_21.
- **The Klein quartic and the projective plane are drawn as themselves.** The Klein quartic showed a 14-sided figure with its sides paired the wrong way and its curved edges only roughly drawn; it is now the true hyperbolic 14-gon with sides glued as Klein gave them. The projective plane was an open band; it is now Boy's surface, a closed surface that passes through itself, the classic picture of the projective plane in space.
