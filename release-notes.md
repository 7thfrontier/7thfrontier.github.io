You can leave a variation sheet, and videos play smoothly at their frame rate · 2026-10-25

- **You can leave a variation sheet.** Press Escape, or choose Close sheet in the corner of the canvas, to go back to the picture you had before the sheet; Undo brings the sheet back. On a Buddhabrot sheet Undo works again too: while the cells refined, each Undo landed back on the sheet.
- **Videos play smoothly at their frame rate.** Record Video and Frame-Exact Export saved each frame for as long as it took to render, so a clip sped up through quick frames and stuttered on slow ones, such as a deep zoom or a density fractal, and could drop a frame. Each frame now lasts one frame at the chosen rate, however long it took to draw. A video export now takes at least as long as the clip itself.
