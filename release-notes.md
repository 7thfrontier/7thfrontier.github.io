Choose the export's aspect ratio · 2026-11-02

- **A turned view stays on WebGPU.** With View Rotation set, an escape-time fractal renders on WebGPU as an unturned one does, and the engine badge no longer reports a WebGL2 fallback for the turn.
- **Report a bug from the website.** The AEON site links to a bug form on GitHub, which asks for your version, your browser and system, and the steps that lead to the problem.
- **Choose the export's aspect ratio.** The Export modal has an Aspect row under Resolution: 1:1 (the default), 16:9, 16:10, 21:9, 3:2, 4:3, 9:16 or a custom width to height. The Resolution preset becomes the long edge, so 4K at 16:9 saves 4096 × 2304. A fractal fills the wider or taller frame with more of the plane at the same center and zoom, with no bars and no stretching, and so do Layer Studio composites and supersampled exports from Print options. Match canvas and Custom size keep their own shape, and picking an aspect under either switches to the nearest preset. Motion exports keep their own Screen, Square, HD and Vertical sizes for now.
