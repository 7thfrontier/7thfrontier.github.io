Aperiodic tilings drawn true · 2026-10-07

- **The Hat draws the real hat tiling.** The Aperiodic Hat pattern used to repeat a 13-sided shape that was not quite the hat on a regular lattice, overlapping its neighbors. It now draws a patch of the true tiling, built by the substitution from the paper: every tile is the same hat, they meet edge to edge with no gaps or overlaps, and about one in eight is a mirror image. The construction is ported from Craig Kaplan's hatviz under the BSD 3-Clause License, whose notice ships in THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md.
- **The Spectre draws the real Spectre tiling.** The Spectre pattern used to repeat rotated copies of the tile on a hexagonal lattice, overlapping. It now draws a patch of the true tiling, built by the paper's two substitution rules: every tile has the same handedness and they meet with no gaps or overlaps. The Edge curvature slider now bends every edge into the same S-curve, so curved tiles still fit their neighbors.
- **The Torus Knot opens as a knot.** Its default was p = 3, q = 3, which is a link of three separate rings rather than a knot. q now starts at 2, so the first picture is the trefoil (3, 2), and the Inspire pick uses p = 5. A link still appears whenever p and q share a factor, as the note explains.
- **Three star 4-polytopes draw their faces and cells.** The Icosahedral 120-cell, the Great Icosahedral 120-cell and the Grand 600-cell used to draw extra or wrong edges to look different from one another. Each now draws its true 720 edges and 1200 triangular faces, with one whole cell solid in the middle: an icosahedron, a great icosahedron or a tetrahedron. Two of them share every edge and face, and the solid cell is what tells them apart. Their notes now say this.
- **Three more star 4-polytopes draw their faces and cells.** The Great 120-cell, the Grand 120-cell and the Great Grand 120-cell now draw their true edges and 720 pentagonal faces, with one whole cell solid: a great dodecahedron or a dodecahedron. The Great and Grand 120-cells share every edge, and the solid cell tells them apart. The Great Grand 120-cell used to draw 1800 edges where it has 1200.
- **The last three star 4-polytopes draw their faces and cells.** The Small Stellated, Great Stellated and Grand Stellated 120-cells now draw their true edges and 720 pentagram faces, with one whole cell solid: a small or a great stellated dodecahedron. All nine star polytopes on the 600-cell's vertices are now drawn exactly. The note on the 600-cell long-chord wireframe no longer says the tenth, {5/2, 3, 3}, shares a wireframe here: it has the 120-cell's 600 vertices. On all nine the faces show, faintly, when Fill is on; the solid cell always shows, and each draws faster than before.
- **Penrose P1 draws the real pentagon tiling.** The entry showed one small fixed cluster of tiles. It now draws the 1974 tiling of pentagons, stars, boats and diamonds across the frame, built from the rhombus tiling it is locally equivalent to: three pentagons on each fat half-rhombus, with the other shapes filling the gaps. Each pentagon contains Penrose’s own six-pentagon dissection two levels down, and P1 gains the Depth control P2 and P3 already had.
