The geometry catalog keeps only figures you can shape, and eight new ones join it · 2026-11-27

- **Typing a command name in Cmd-K lists that command first.** Typing "export" listed figures such as Quat. Julia above Export image, because the catalog was always shown before the commands and a figure's search words could hold the typed letters scattered through them. Results now come in the order of how well they match, so "export", "gallery", "reset" and "save" each put their command first, and Enter runs it.
- **Fill Opacity on the polyhedra now shows with Fill faces.** On Uniform Polyhedron, the four star polyhedra and Composite Polyhedron, the slider used to follow the Fill switch, which these solids do not use, so it showed when it changed nothing and hid when the faces were filled. It now appears while Fill faces is on.
- **Eight new figures, each one shaped by its own controls.** Guilloche Rosette, Moiré, Times-Table Circle, Pursuit Whirl, Doyle Spiral, Spirolateral, Frieze Band and Loxodrome Sphere join the catalog, each with its own sliders for its form.
- **They sit where they belong in the Catalog.** Times-Table and Spirolateral join Number Theory, Moiré, Pursuit Whirl and Doyle Spiral join Algorithmic, Guilloche joins Algebraic Curves, Frieze and Loxodrome join Topology & Symmetry. Each has a preview, a page in the figure's info, and a Random params roll that stays inside shapes worth looking at.
- **Cyclic CA has States and Threshold sliders, and Butterfly Curve has a Wings slider.** Fewer states grow large spirals and more break them into turbulence; a higher threshold holds cells back longer. Wings folds the butterfly into 2 to 8 wings, with 4 drawing the classic curve.
- **Composite Polyhedron has a Copies menu.** It repeats the outer solid 2, 5 or 10 times, through the center and around a fivefold axis, so a tetrahedron makes the Stella Octangula, the compound of five tetrahedra and the compound of ten tetrahedra, and a cube the compound of five cubes. The ten tetrahedra are also a new preset.
- **Exports carry their provenance in their own file metadata.** JPEG, TIFF, EXR, GIF and SVG exports, and WebM video the app encodes itself, now record the app version, what was drawn, the pixel size, the renderers and the link that reopens the piece, as PNG exports already did. `reactor-render` writes the same fields into its PNG and JPEG files. Formats the browser writes as finished files, such as WebP and MP4, are unchanged.
- **`reactor-render --state` renders a saved piece at full size.** It opens a .aeon project, a share link (or a file holding one) or a PNG the app exported, the way the app does, and saves it through the app's own export, so layers, blend, opacity, Transparent background, keyframes and the saved export settings all come along. `--size` sets the image size (3840x2160 by default), and the image is rendered at that size, never resized. If the export would come out at any other size, nothing is written.
- **Egg of Life and Fruit of Life are now figures of Flower of Life.** Pick them from the new Figure menu beside Flower of Life's 3D switch; each draws exactly as before, in 2D and in 3D, and its Info and Construction notes are its own.
- **Sona is now a style of Kolam, and Kolam has an Extra columns slider.** Kolam's Style menu holds the Tamil kolam and the Chokwe sona, and an Extra columns value that shares no factor with the rows draws one unbroken line. Kolam's rows now go up to 12.
- **Spherical Icosahedron and Spherical Dodecahedron are now Geodesic Polyhedron at Frequency 1, and the dome has a Dual switch.** Frequency 1 draws the spherical icosahedron, and with Dual on the spherical dodecahedron, exactly as the two figures did. At higher frequencies Dual turns the triangle dome into its Goldberg polyhedron: 12 pentagons and a growing number of hexagons, the pattern of a football or a fullerene molecule. Each one's Info is its own. The Spherical & Elliptic section, left empty, is gone.
- **Stella Octangula and the compounds of five tetrahedra and five cubes are now presets of Composite Polyhedron.** Pick them from its Preset menu: each draws exactly as before, on its own, and its Info is its own.
- **The 5 Platonic, 13 Archimedean and 13 Catalan solids are now one figure, Uniform Polyhedron.** Its sliders reach each of the 31 solids and every shape between them, and Info names the solid when they land on one. The dodecahedron and four of its relatives now sit a quarter turn around.
- **The Hat and the Spectre are now one figure, Aperiodic Monotile, with a Shape slider.** Shape runs the tile through its whole family, Tile(a,b): 60° is the hat, 30° the turtle and 45° the Spectre, which keeps its Edge curvature. Every angle between is the same aperiodic tiling with the tile's two kinds of edge stretched to a new ratio, and the ends, 0° and 90°, are the comet and the chevron. The hat and the Spectre draw exactly as they did, and Info names whichever tile Shape is on.
- **Penrose Tiling now holds all three Penrose tilings in its Tile set.** Pick the pentagons, stars, boats and diamonds of P1, the kite and dart of P2 or the thick and thin rhombi of P3 from its new Tile set menu; each draws exactly as before, Depth subdivides it, and its Info is its own. The default stays the rhombus tiling colored around the center. A link opens a figure at one of its members with &set=, as the landing page's kite-and-dart tile now does with ?pattern=penrose&set=p2.
- **The geometry catalog now holds 99 figures,** after the merges above and the eight new figures.
- **Hypocycloid no longer draws a small faint circle at its center.** It was a construction guide, not part of the figure.
- **The geometry catalog keeps only figures you can shape and make into art: 50 are gone.** Each one had no controls that change its form or was a diagram that needs a lesson, such as the Cayley graphs, wire globes, gluing polygons, lone textbook curves and number charts.
- **Six were duplicates of figures that stay, and you can still draw them there.** Seed of Life is Flower of Life at one ring, Deltoid and Astroid are Hypocycloid at 3 and 4 petals, Cardioid is Epicycloid at 1 petal, Lemniscate is Cassini Ovals at ratio 1 (searching "lemniscate" or "figure eight" now finds it), and Trefoil Knot is Torus Knot at (2,3).
- **The Music Mathematics and Group Theory & Topology sections are gone.** Hopf Fibration, the one figure left from the second, moved to Topology & Symmetry. A share link or bookmark that names a removed figure no longer opens it, and a message says so; an old bookmark of one used to switch nothing while reporting it loaded.
