Density variation sheets answer you while they render, and videos keep transparent layers · 2026-10-21

- **A density variation sheet keeps sharpening while you work.** Each cell of a Buddhabrot, attractor, IFS or flame sheet now picks up its full-quality render where it left off when you click, type or scroll, instead of starting over, so the cells finish even while you keep interacting, and each still ends exactly as an uninterrupted render would.
- **An export made while a variation sheet refines its cells no longer fails.** If an export, or a layer render, started while a density cell (an attractor, IFS fractal, flame or Buddhabrot) was still rendering at full samples, both could keep interrupting each other until the export failed with "The render was interrupted by a new render twice in a row". Layer renders now take turns.
- **A variation sheet of a density fractal, such as a Buddhabrot or a flame, answers you while it renders.** Its cells appear within a second or two as a grainier draft, then each one finishes at full quality in the background. Clicks and keys take effect at once, and the cells pick up again when you pause. Before, the app ignored input for up to 15 seconds while the cells rendered.
- **A multi-layer export no longer inflates the layer previews.** If the layer previews refilled while a multi-layer export ran, they were rendered at the export's size, up to hundreds of megabytes a layer for a large print, and then rendered again at screen size. The previews now wait for the export and render at screen size.
- **A video or PNG sequence keeps a layer's transparent background.** In a composite whose selected layer has a transparent background, Record Video, Frame-Exact and PNG-sequence exports drew that layer opaque, hiding the layers beneath it, though a still export and the screen showed them. Each frame now matches a still export of the same moment.
