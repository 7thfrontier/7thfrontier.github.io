A first visit says what to do · 2026-10-07

- **A first visit says what to do on the first frame.** The welcome used to open as a small dialog over a dimmed picture: it said what AEON is but not what to try, and the steps only arrived in a second card once it closed. Now one guide sits beside the live picture from the start, with three moves to try: zoom in, try another form, change the colors. Each one checks off as you do it, and the control you need is outlined. If the picture has not been touched after 15 seconds, a marker points at it. The one-minute video and Tour the controls are in the guide too. Hide folds it to a small Getting started chip that remembers your progress. The wordmark and the Guided tours menu open it again, and the app menu's Help group now holds Showcase, Foreword, References and What's new.
- **The one-minute tour plays inside AEON.** Watch the 1-minute tour now plays in AEON itself, with captions, from the app's own files: it works offline in the download and contacts no other site. The landing page plays the same video.
- **Catalog previews appear where you are looking.** Previews render one at a time, and every card you scrolled past used to stay in line ahead of the ones on screen, so scrolling down to a section such as Group Theory & Topology could leave it blank for many seconds. A card that has left the view now waits until you scroll back to it.
