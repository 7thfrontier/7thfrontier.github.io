A share link of a single layer keeps its position, rotation, opacity and blend · 2026-11-16

- **A share link of a single layer now keeps the layer's position, rotation, opacity, blend and transparent background.** Sharing one used to point you to Save Project instead; two or more layers still do, and links made earlier open as they always did. A hand-edited link can no longer open the layer off the canvas: its position and size stay within what the app itself writes.
