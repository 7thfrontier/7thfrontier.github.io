Fill controls only where a pattern fills · 2026-11-08

- **The Opacity Field's Fill controls no longer appear on patterns that draw strokes only.** On 115 such patterns the Fill Field could be set and nothing changed, while its sliders sat greyed with nothing able to wake them. They are now hidden there, as Fill Opacity, Fill Rule and Fill Blend already were, and they return when you switch to a pattern that fills. Turning on 3D for Phyllotaxis, which fills in 2D but not in 3D, now updates the Fill controls straight away instead of on the next pattern change.
