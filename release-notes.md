Every line of a pattern shows · 2026-10-07

- **Sacred-geometry lines no longer vanish into the background.** Most palettes start near black, so the first lines of a pattern drew almost exactly the canvas color: the Seed of Life showed six circles instead of seven, and parts of Metatron's Cube, the mandala and the Penrose tiling went missing. A line too faint to see now takes the nearest color in the palette that reads against the background, on a dark or a light canvas. Every other line keeps its color, and cellular automata and other cell patterns keep their dark tones.
- **The Sri Yantra draws all nine triangles.** Picking it from the catalog drew seven of its nine triangles; it now draws the full set of four upward and five downward. The Rigorous option is gone: it claimed a construction it did not draw, and the description now says plainly that the figure is a stylized version of the diagram.
- **A link to an IFS, geometry or density view opens at its final size.** Opening a share link or a URL for one of those views drew the picture at the window's width for a moment, then widened it once the app had loaded. The picture now starts at its final size.
