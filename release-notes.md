The guide comes first on a slow start · 2026-11-11

- **On a slow computer the toolbar no longer appears before the guide.** A device slow to start the app could show the toolbar and panels for a couple of seconds before the getting-started guide, and their controls did nothing yet. They now stay hidden until the app is ready, then appear together with the guide.
