Strange attractors can be colored by orbit density · 2026-11-10

- **Strange attractors can be colored by orbit density.** Attractor Parameters has a new Color by choice: Angle, the default, colors each point by its angle about the origin as before, and Density runs the attractor's log-density brightness through the palette, so the dense core and the sparse halo take opposite ends of it. It applies to the live view, Animate emergence and exports, and travels in share links.
