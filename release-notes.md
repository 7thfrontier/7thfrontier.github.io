Video exports keep the whole view · 2026-10-30

- **Video exports keep the whole view when their shape differs from the screen.** A square or vertical video of a wide fractal view used to fill the frame and lose the sides of the picture, which still exports stopped doing in 7.0.4. Record Video, PNG Sequence and Frame-Exact now follow the same Fractal Compose choice as stills: Letterbox, the default, keeps the whole view on bars of the background color, and Extend fills the frame as before. The Letterbox and Extend chips now also sit in the Motion panel, under the resolution chips.
