A Mandelbulb or Quaternion Julia set now uses its whole palette · 2026-10-27

- **A Mandelbulb or Quaternion Julia set now uses its whole palette.** Their surfaces took color from only the first eighth or so of the chosen palette, so a rainbow palette rendered red to orange and most of any palette never showed. The color now spreads across the palette the way it already did on the Mandelbox, Menger sponge and Sierpinski tetrahedron, and the color scale no longer depends on Max Iterations. On a 3D layer with Transparent background, a half-transparent palette stop also no longer shows a bright surface brighter than half its opaque color. The fix now reaches the Quaternion Julia set under WebGPU too. Max Iterations is hidden for the Quaternion Julia set, Menger sponge and Sierpinski tetrahedron, where it no longer changes the picture, and on the Mandelbulb and Mandelbox its hint says they read at most 256 iterations.
