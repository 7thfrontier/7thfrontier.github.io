A layer stays where its sliders say, however it got there · 2026-11-18

- **A layer now stays where its sliders say, however it got there.** 7.11.12 said a hand-edited share link could no longer open a layer off the canvas, and that was not yet true: a link, a drag of the move handle or an opened .aeon file could still put a layer's position past the X and Y sliders' ends or its size past the Scale slider's, while the sliders showed their ends. All of them now keep a layer within what the sliders show.
- **A layer's Move, Scale and Rotate fields show the value the layer actually takes.** Typing a number past a field's range, such as 400 in Rotate, and pressing Tab or Enter turned the layer only as far as the range allows (180) while the field went on reading 400. The field now changes to the value in use, and a field emptied and left goes back to it.
