The status bar shows the version you are running, and the render chip stays put · 2026-10-19

- **The render chip on the status bar no longer vanishes when clicked.** It reports what draws the picture, at what precision, and whether it is finished, and it stays on the bar for the whole session.
- **The status bar shows the version you are running.** Its last cell reads the version number, and hovering it names the build it came from, so a screenshot of a problem says which build it was taken on.
