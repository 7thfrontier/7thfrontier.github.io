Variation sheets fill every cell, and the status bar fits a narrow window · 2026-10-21

- **The status bar stays inside its frame in a narrow window.** At about 900 px wide, the bar's end cells, the version among them, ran past both of its edges once the version label gained a digit. The coloring algorithm readout now folds into More with the other optional readouts when room runs out, and a bar that still cannot fit wraps onto a second row instead of running off.
- **A variation sheet of a strange attractor or an IFS fractal fills every cell.** It used to stop after the first cell and show a warning about an interrupted export, though nothing was being exported. A cell that does fail now leaves only that cell empty, and the export warning appears only during an export.
- **A focused status-bar cell keeps keyboard focus when the window narrows.** When a narrower window moved the status bar out of the bottom strip, or folded a focused Raise Max Iter or WebGPU badge into the closed More menu, focus fell back to the page. Focus now stays on the cell, or moves to More when the cell is folded away.
- **Inspector controls keep keyboard focus while the inspector sets up.** When the inspector grouped its sections during startup, a control that already had keyboard focus lost it to the page. It now keeps focus.
- **Captures wait for a deep zoom rendered on the CPU.** When a deep view had to render on the CPU (with orbit traps or a shaded interior on, or on a GPU that fails the precision check), the app reported the render finished while it was still computing, so a gallery thumbnail or a duplicated layer could keep the previous frame. Captures now wait for the new frame.
- **The Center field takes more ways of writing a point.** Typing a center into the coordinate bar now accepts `.5` without a leading zero, a leading `+`, the `−` minus sign the app itself displays, and a pair in parentheses such as `(-0.5, 0.6)`. Deep-zoom centers keep every typed digit.
- **Catalog search treats a hyphen and a space alike, and Cmd-K finds types by concept.** Typing "reaction-diffusion", "escape time" or "space-filling" in the catalog now finds the types it names, whichever separator you use. Cmd-K's Types and Patterns also search the same concept words as the catalog, so "strange attractor", "cellular" or "sacred geometry" list their types there too, and Cmd-K matches a hyphen and a space alike, so "high contrast" finds Toggle high-contrast mode.
- **The new-version dot moves to the Application menu.** The dot that says a new version is out now sits on the Application menu button, and the menu marks its What's new item as New. Opening What's new from there clears both. The AEON wordmark no longer carries the dot, since clicking it opens the guide and never cleared it; it still turns when you hover it. The "A new version is available." banner gains a What's New button beside Reload: it reloads the same way, then opens the notes for the version you just loaded.
- **The zoom readout and keyframe labels read consistently.** A zoom just under 10 or 1000 now reads "10×" or "1.0e+3×" like its neighbors instead of "10.0×" or "1000×", and a zoom under 1 keeps two decimals, so a 3D attractor zoomed fully out reads "0.05×" rather than "0.1×". In Motion Studio, a keyframe whose Julia constant is negative shows a true minus sign in its list row, matching the center beside it.
