Palette transparency lets the layer below show through a 3D fractal layer with a transparent background · 2026-10-27

- **Palette transparency lets a lower layer show through a 3D fractal layer with Transparent background.** On a Mandelbulb or another 3D fractal, a palette stop with partial opacity, or an Opacity Field, used to fade the surface toward the background color while the surface stayed opaque. On a layer with Transparent background on, the surface is now see-through by the same amount, so the layer beneath shows through it, on screen and in exports. The empty space around a 3D fractal on such a layer is now fully clear too: it used to lighten the layer beneath with a faint blue-grey, brighter near the fractal's glow. With the box off, and with a fully opaque palette, the fractal itself looks the same as before.
