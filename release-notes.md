A fractal layer's transparent background lets the layer below show through the inside of the set · 2026-10-26

- **Transparent background now works on escape-time fractals such as Multibrot.** Ticking it on one of these layers used to change nothing, because they color every pixel. Now the inside of the set, drawn in the Interior color, turns transparent, so a Flower of Life placed beneath a Multibrot shows inside the set and nowhere else, on screen and in exports. The Export dialog offers Transparent background for these types too.
- **A Buddhabrot variation sheet sharpens right away again.** Since 7.3.13 its cells could stay at their quick, grainy picture for tens of seconds after the sheet appeared, while the main picture was drawn again with nothing changed. The cells now start refining as soon as the sheet is up, even while you keep working.
